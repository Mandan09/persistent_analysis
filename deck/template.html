What to tell leadership

  • 68% is right for new, continuously insured patients who are allowed 60-day breaks.
  • It is not "share of prescribed patients who stay on": with 30-day breaks and never-filled prescriptions it is nearer .
  • Pick the rule that matches the decision, and show the curve, not one point.
